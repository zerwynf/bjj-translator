Guarda

Guard

GWAR-dah

Category

Positions

A fundamental ground position where you control your opponent using your legs. The guard is considered the foundation of BJJ and includes many variations such as closed guard, open guard, and half guard.

Usage: "Puxar para guarda" — Pull to guard
